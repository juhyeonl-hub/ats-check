Backend Engineer
Maple Gate builds logistics planning software for merchants and warehouses.
The role is implementation-heavy, with a mix of feature work and cleanup.

Requirements:
- Java, Spring Boot, Kotlin, and Kubernetes are expected in the backend stack.
- PostgreSQL, Docker, and REST APIs are used every week.
- Clear English for written decisions and support handovers.
- You can keep small releases moving without hiding unresolved questions.

Nice to have:
- Terraform is a plus for infrastructure changes.
- Interest in supply chain operations is appreciated.

Benefits:
- Remote-friendly work from Finland or nearby EU countries.
- Lunch benefit, wellness budget, and a practical equipment policy.
